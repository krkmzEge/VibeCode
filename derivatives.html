Recall sheet

A quick refresher with the language used in the main notes.

Power Rule

If f(x) = xⁿ, then f′(x) = n·xⁿ⁻¹. Use it any time exponents are plain numbers.

Product Rule

(uv)' = u'v + uv'. Think “front derivative times back, plus back derivative times front.”

Chain Rule

Differentiate the outside, keep the inside, then multiply by the inside’s derivative. Perfect for compositions.

Quotient Rule

(u/v)' = (u'v - uv') / v². Or rewrite as product with v⁻¹ if that’s easier.

Warm-up

Differentiate f(x) = 3x⁴ - 2x² + 1

Answer: f′(x) = 12x³ - 4x.

Product + Chain

f(x) = e^{x²} · sin(3x)

Answer: f′(x) = 2x e^{x²} sin(3x) + 3 e^{x²} cos(3x).

Quotient

f(x) = (x² + 1)/(x - 1)

Answer: f′(x) = [(2x)(x-1) - (x²+1)] / (x-1)².

Chain

f(x) = (4x³ - x)⁵

Answer: f′(x) = 5(4x³ - x)⁴ · (12x² - 1).
