Differential Equations Lab

The slope-field animation on the home page shows particles aligning with the equation \( y' = \sin x + \cos y \). These notes explain how to interpret that flow and how to sketch solutions by eye before solving algebraically.

Decode the slope field

Each short dash in the visualization is a miniature tangent line representing the derivative at that point. Read them like arrows pointing along your future solution curve.

Direction

If the dash tilts upward, the derivative is positive; downward means negative. Horizontal segments mark critical points.

Density

Areas with tight parallel dashes show stable regions where many solutions travel together.

Color cue

In the interactive page the highlighted solution glows orange so you can track a single integral curve.

Sketching workflow before solving

01

Pick a starting point

Choose any coordinate and imagine a particle dropped there. Its slope is given directly by the nearby dashes.

02

Follow the field

Move a short distance following the local slope, then adjust to match the next dash. Repeat for a hand-drawn solution.

03

Compare with algebra

After solving \( y' = \sin x + \cos y \) analytically, overlay the function to check that it rides along the same stream.
