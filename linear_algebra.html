Example walkthrough

A short sample from the notes showing how one row-column product corresponds to the new x-value of the grid.

{(2, 1)} · {(4), (-2)} = (2 × 4) + (1 × -2) = 8 - 2 = 6
{(2, 1)} · {(1), (5)} = (2 × 1) + (1 × 5) = 2 + 5 = 7

The animation highlights the active row and column so the numerical pattern above literally glows on the grid.
